## Examples

### Example 1

**Input:** `expertise = [0, 0, 0, 1]`
**Input:** `skill = [10, 2, 3, 4]`

**Output:** `7`

**Explanation:** The optimal selection is [3, 4] with 1 marketing expert and 1 developer. The sum of skills is 3 + 4 = 7. Hence the answer is 7.
