### Example 1

**Input:** `security = [2, 3, 3, 3, 2, 1]`

**Output:** `4`

**Explanation:** Consider n = 6 and security = [2, 3, 3, 3, 2, 1].Then, the elements can be grouped as follows:Group 1: 2 devices of vulnerability 2.Group 2: 2 devices of vulnerability 3.Group 3: 1 device of vulnerability 3.Group 4: 1 device of vulnerability 1.It requires 4 groups.
